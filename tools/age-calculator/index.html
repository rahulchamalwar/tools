Age Calculator

Calculate a precise age as of today, including completed years, months and days. The date is calculated locally in your browser.

How to use this tool

  1. Choose your date of birth.
  2. Select Calculate age.
  3. Review your age and total days lived.

Features

  • Calendar-aware age breakdown
  • Total months and days
  • Future date validation

Frequently asked questions

Is this tool free to use?

Yes. Open Lab Tools is free to use, with no account required.

Are my files uploaded?

File tools process your selected files in your browser. The files are not intentionally sent to an Open Lab Tools server.

Can I use this tool on my phone?

Yes. The page is responsive and works in current mobile and desktop browsers.
