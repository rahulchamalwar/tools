EMI Calculator

Plan a loan with a clear estimate of its monthly payment and lifetime cost. Adjust the interest rate or term to compare scenarios before you make a decision.

How to use this tool

  1. Enter the loan amount and annual interest rate.
  2. Choose a tenure in months or years, then select Calculate.
  3. Review your EMI, total interest and monthly amortization schedule.

Features

  • Monthly EMI and total repayment estimate
  • Principal and interest breakdown
  • Month-by-month amortization table

Frequently asked questions

Is this tool free to use?

Yes. Open Lab Tools is free to use, with no account required.

Are my files uploaded?

File tools process your selected files in your browser. The files are not intentionally sent to an Open Lab Tools server.

Can I use this tool on my phone?

Yes. The page is responsive and works in current mobile and desktop browsers.
