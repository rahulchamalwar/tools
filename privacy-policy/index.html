Privacy Policy

Read how Open Lab Tools handles information and browser-based file processing.

Open Lab Tools does not require an account to use its calculators and utilities. The site does not intentionally store files selected in browser-based tools.

Selected images, PDFs, text and passwords are processed in your browser whenever technically possible. The site does not receive those files through the tool interface.

A production deployment may use hosting logs, analytics or advertising services if configured by the site owner. Configure consent, retention and disclosures for any analytics or advertising vendors before enabling them.

Do not enter information into a browser tool if you are not comfortable processing it on your device. This policy should be reviewed for the actual hosting, analytics and advertising configuration before launch.
